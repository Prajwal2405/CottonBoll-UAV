Axis definition (added for the public release, 2026-11-19; reproduces the split above exactly)
  u is the along-track coordinate, per field, in metres:
  1. Project each frame's GPS position to a local flat-earth plane about the field's mean
     position: east = (lon - lon0) * 111320 * cos(lat0), north = (lat - lat0) * 111320.
  2. Flight axis = median direction (mod 180 deg) of the displacement between consecutive
     frames in capture order (pre: 2.31 deg, post: 101.08 deg, counter-clockwise from east).
  3. u = position projected onto that axis (centred on the field mean).
  With this definition every frame is reassigned to its listed split (0 mismatches) and the
  minimum along-track test<->train separation is 13.04 m (pre) and 13.05 m (post).
  Implied strip limits: pre u <= -60.59 / u >= 62.17; post u <= -59.53 / u >= 58.67
